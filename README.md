## 1. Project Overview & Problem Statement

### The Problem
Conventional charitable fundraising platforms suffer from opacity, central points of failure, intermediary fees, and a lack of accountability. Donors have no cryptographic guarantee that their contributions are held in secure escrow, whether campaigns achieve their minimum viable budget, or whether unfulfilled campaigns return funds to contributors.

### The Solution: TrustLedger
**TrustLedger** is a decentralized application (dApp) engineered on the Ethereum blockchain. It replaces opaque, discretionary management with transparent Solidity smart contracts:
- **Immutable Ledger:** All campaigns, donations, and withdrawals are permanently recorded on-chain.
- **Milestone & Goal-Enforced Escrow:** Campaign creators cannot withdraw funds unless 100% of the funding goal is met.
- **Automated Donor Refunds:** If a campaign's deadline expires without reaching its goal, donors can trustlessly reclaim their exact contributions directly from the contract.
- **Platform Verification Gatekeeping:** Only campaigns vetted and verified by the platform administrator can accept incoming contributions.

## 6. Functional Workflows

### 1. Campaign Creation
- Any connected account can navigate to **Create Campaign**.
- Enter Title, Description, Funding Goal (in ETH), and Duration.
- Click **Deploy Campaign to Blockchain** and confirm via MetaMask.
- The contract emits `CampaignCreated` and registers the campaign as **unverified**.

### 2. Campaign Verification (Admin Gatekeeping)
- Connect with Account #0 (Platform Administrator).
- Open the unverified campaign. An exclusive **Verify Campaign Now** action appears.
- Approving this transaction marks the campaign as verified on-chain, unlocking donations.

### 3. Transparent Donations
- Connect with any donor account (e.g., Account #1).
- Open any verified campaign, input an ETH amount, and click **Confirm Donation**.
- The contract safely records the contribution, increments `totalRaised`, emits `DonationReceived`, and updates the public donation ledger.

### 4. Creator Fund Withdrawal
- Once a campaign reaches 100% of its funding goal (`totalRaised >= fundingGoal`), the creator connects their wallet.
- The **Withdraw Funds** button becomes available.
- Funds are transferred using the Checks-Effects-Interactions pattern, and the campaign is closed.

### 5. Automated Donor Refunds
- If a campaign expires before achieving its funding target, donors can open the campaign or check **My Donations**.
- The contract enables **Claim Refund**, instantly sending 100% of their contribution back to their wallet and resetting their recorded balance to zero.

## 8. Security Considerations

- **Checks-Effects-Interactions Pattern:** All state modifications occur before Ether is transferred to prevent reentrancy attacks.
- **Pull over Push Payments:** Refunds and withdrawals require recipients to initiate the transaction ("pull"), preventing Denial-of-Service (DoS) attacks caused by failing external transfers.
- **Input Sanitization:** String lengths and numeric values are strictly checked in Solidity.
- **Strict Role-Based Access Control:** Critical administrative functions are protected by `onlyAdmin` modifiers.

## 9. Educational Disclaimer & Real-World Limitations

- **Educational Purpose:** This project is built as an educational demonstration of blockchain technology and smart contract engineering. It uses test ETH on a local Hardhat network.
- **Verification Scope:** An on-chain `verified` status proves only that an administrator approved the campaign in the application. It does not certify legal non-profit status or physically audit off-chain operations.
- **Post-Withdrawal Tracking:** The blockchain guarantees transparent escrow up to withdrawal; monitoring physical fund expenditure in the real world requires off-chain auditing, milestone attestations, and legal oversight.
